How is heat calculated?

Each item is scored by rules: base 50 points; freshness (+25 within 6h, +20 within 24h, +10 within 72h); Official sources +10; T3 sources -15; matching topic keywords (tutorial, workflow, case study, guide, tool, data, review, privacy, security, model, performance, subscription, call, Spaces, voice, watch, open source, prompt) +5 each, capped at +25; noise keywords -5 each, floor -15; total capped at 100.
